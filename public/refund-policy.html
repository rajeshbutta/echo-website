Refund Policy

Effective date: 1 October 2026  ·  Published by Saral Technomart Private Limited

Echo CRM offers a 90-day free trial with no credit card required. You are only charged after you choose a paid plan.

Overview

We want you to be completely satisfied with Echo CRM. This Refund Policy describes when and how refunds are issued for paid subscriptions.

Free Trial

All new accounts receive a 90-day free trial with full access to all features. No payment information is required during the trial. If you choose not to continue after the trial, simply stop using the Service — no action and no refund request is needed.

Monthly Subscriptions

If you cancel a monthly subscription within 7 days of a billing date and have not actively used the Service during that billing period, you may request a full refund for that month's charge.

Cancellations after 7 days will take effect at the end of the current billing period. No partial-month refunds are issued.

Annual Subscriptions

If you cancel an annual subscription within 30 days of payment and have not actively used the Service during that period, you may request a full refund.

After 30 days, annual subscriptions are non-refundable. You will retain access to the Service for the remainder of the paid period.

Service Disruptions

If the Service experiences a verified outage of more than 24 consecutive hours due to factors within our control, affected users may be eligible for a pro-rated credit applied to the next billing cycle. Credits are not transferable and have no cash value.

Non-Refundable Items

  • Charges for completed billing periods beyond the refund window
  • Add-on usage fees (e.g., additional calling minutes beyond the plan limit)
  • Accounts terminated for violation of our Terms of Service
  • Downgrade from a higher to a lower plan mid-cycle

How to Request a Refund

To request a refund, email us at support@avionsoftware.in with:

  • Your registered email address
  • The date of the charge
  • The reason for the refund request

We will review and respond within 5 business days. Approved refunds are processed within 7–10 business days to the original payment method.
