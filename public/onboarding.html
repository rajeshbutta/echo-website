Onboarding

Get up and running in 6 steps

1

Sign in with your phone number

Visit admin.saraltechnomart.com. Enter your mobile number — you'll receive a 6-digit OTP to verify. No password needed.

OTP expires in 5 minutes
2

Set up your organisation

On first login, you'll be prompted to create your organisation profile — enter your firm name. This becomes the sender's name in all client communications.

3

Add your team

Go to Team in the sidebar. Add your team members by clicking the Add Member button in the top right corner. Assign roles — Admin has full access; Advisor sees only their own clients and activities.

Admin-only feature
4

Import your clients & policies

Go to Renewals → Import. Download the CSV template, fill in your client and policy data, then upload.

Mandatory columns: Client Name, Mobile, Product Category, Product Name. Once imported, open each client product to set the renewal schedule — reference number, provider, premium amount, periodicity, and next due date.

Currently enabled for insurance advisors. For those managing loans, investments, or other recurring products, this feature is being adapted — we'd love your feedback on what works best.

Bulk upload supported
5

Configure reminder rules

Go to Renewals → Comm Settings. Choose how many days before a due date to send reminders (e.g. 30, 7, 1 day) and which channels — WhatsApp or Email or both.

6

You're live — reminders run automatically

Every morning at 8:00 AM IST, Echo sends reminders to clients whose policies match your Comm Settings rules. View the full log under Renewals → Reminder Activity.

No daily action needed

Good to Know

A few things before you start

Unique Mobile Numbers

Within your organisation, every client (lead or customer) and every team member must have a unique mobile number. Duplicate mobiles are not allowed in the system.

Policy / Account Numbers

Every policy or product entry must have a unique Policy / Account Number. This is how Echo prevents duplicate imports and accurately tracks each product over time.

Reminder Emails

Outgoing reminder emails are sent from your own domain once you verify a sender address in Comm Settings → Email. Until verified, reminders go from Echo's default address.

WhatsApp Setup

WhatsApp reminders require a META WhatsApp Business Account (WABA). Our team will set this up with you on a brief call, since your Facebook Business profile is used to create the WABA account.

Calling Numbers

Echo assigns your organisation one or more virtual calling numbers through Plivo. You can configure multiple numbers — for example, assign a dedicated number to each advisor or to different teams and territories. All calls made through the app show your organisation's virtual number as the caller ID, keeping personal numbers private.

AI Call Transcription

Every call made through Echo is automatically recorded and processed by AI. You get a full transcript, a summary, and key action points — available to both the advisor and the admin.

Recordings are retained for 60 days. Transcripts and summaries are stored permanently in your account.

Call Recording — Your Responsibility

Calls are recorded silently without an automated announcement. Advisors must inform the client at the start of every call that the call will be recorded. This is a legal and ethical requirement in most jurisdictions.

Platform Availability

Echo CRM is available as an Android app for advisors on the go. Apple (iOS) users can access the full application through any mobile browser at admin.saraltechnomart.com — the interface is fully responsive and works on any screen size.

A native iOS app is not available due to Apple's restrictions on third-party telephony integrations.
